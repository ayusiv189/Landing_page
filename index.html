About TruFit

From WhatsApp chaos to one connected sports platform.

TruFit modernizes the everyday journey of finding a venue, getting players together, booking a slot and managing the arena.

✕

The old process

  • Call multiple venues to find an open slot.
  • Coordinate players across messy group chats.
  • Last-minute cancellations and empty spots.
  • Manual booking records and payment tracking.
✓

The TruFit standard

  • Discover turfs and see available slots.
  • Book with a simple digital flow.
  • Join lobbies and find players for the game.
  • Owners manage bookings, dues and venue operations.
